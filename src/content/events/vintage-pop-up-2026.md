michelle tamar is a 90s and Y2K vintage shop with stores in Zurich, Basel and Bern. On 12 September they came to Lucerne for a one-day pop-up together with moebu, the vintage furniture label of Jordi Bucher. It ran from 11:00 to 18:00 at Friedentalstrasse 41A, right behind the Lovers space.

I was there that day to shoot Lovers and asked if I could photograph the pop-up as well. They said yes, and these are the photos from it.
