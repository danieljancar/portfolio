michelle tamar is a 90s and Y2K vintage shop with stores in Zurich, Basel and Bern. On 12 September they came to Lucerne for a one-day pop-up together with moebu, the vintage furniture label. It ran from 11:00 to 18:00 at Friedentalstrasse 41A, right behind the Lovers space and was very well attended.

I was there that day to shoot for Lovers Lovers and by chance asked if I could photograph the pop-up as well. Turned out, that it was a great idea, see for yourself. 😆
